How to Get Involved

The GitOps Working Group is an open group, inviting companies and individuals to join and contribute to the community and the adoption of GitOps across the cloud native landscape. There are a few ways you can get involved:

  • Watch or star OpenGitOps project repo and CNCF Application Delivery TAG: GitOps Working Group folder to see when things change
  • Join the GitOps Subreddit
  • Attend a Working Group or Committee meeting
  • Start or participate in Discussions
  • Open a discussion
  • Open an issue and let us know how you're using GitOps and any important considerations we should include
  • Join #opengitops on CNCF Slack
  • Join the SIG App Delivery mailing list, and watch or participate in topics prefixed with [gitops-wg]
  • Volunteer to join committees and help with projects according to your interest and ability

The Working Group will review all open issues and PRs.
